## Complete Platform Vision

The ambition is much broader than simply creating a property-listing website or construction marketplace.

The platform should ultimately become the **one-stop property, construction and built-environment ecosystem for Ethiopians**, serving customers both **inside Ethiopia and throughout the Ethiopian diaspora internationally**.

The objective is that whenever someone has a need connected to property, construction, housing, maintenance, financing, insurance, employment or professional services, this platform should become one of the first places they think of.

The platform should therefore be architected from the beginning to support the following major business verticals.

### 1. Building Supplies & Construction Materials

A comprehensive construction-materials marketplace covering everything required to build, renovate, repair and maintain residential and commercial property.

This can include:

- Cement
- Reinforcement steel
- Blocks and bricks
- Sand and aggregates
- Roofing materials
- Electrical materials
- Plumbing materials
- Tiles and flooring
- Paint and decorating materials
- Doors and windows
- Sanitary ware
- Kitchens
- Lighting
- Hardware and tools
- Timber
- Construction equipment
- Interior finishing materials
- Landscaping materials
- Safety equipment
- Specialist construction products

The platform should support our own products as well as third-party suppliers and merchants where commercially appropriate.

### 2. Property Marketplace — Ethiopia's Property Portal

Build what should eventually become **Ethiopia's equivalent of Zoopla/Rightmove**, designed specifically around the Ethiopian market.

Users should be able to:

- Buy property
- Sell property
- Rent property
- Let property
- Search new developments
- Search commercial property
- Search land
- Search investment opportunities
- Search by city, sub-city, district and neighbourhood
- Contact agents, developers and owners
- Save properties
- Compare opportunities
- Submit property enquiries
- Request valuations
- Advertise properties

The long-term ambition should be to create one of the most comprehensive databases of Ethiopian property available online.

The platform should serve Ethiopians living domestically as well as diaspora buyers looking to purchase, rent, build or invest in Ethiopia.

### 9. Ethiopian Diaspora Services

This should be treated as an important vertical rather than a small additional feature.

The platform should serve Ethiopians **wherever they live**.

There are potentially two directions.

#### Diaspora → Ethiopia

An Ethiopian living in London, Washington DC, Toronto, Dubai, Stockholm or elsewhere may want help with:

- Buying property in Ethiopia
- Building a house in Ethiopia
- Renovating family property
- Finding contractors
- Purchasing construction materials
- Supervising construction
- Finding architects or engineers
- Maintaining property
- Insurance
- Financing
- Property management
- Investment
- Verification and due diligence

The platform becomes their trusted bridge into the Ethiopian property market.

#### Ethiopian Community → Services Abroad

The platform could also create a marketplace connecting Ethiopians abroad with Ethiopian-owned or Ethiopian-serving property and trade professionals in their country of residence.

For example:

An Ethiopian customer in London needs a plumber.

A qualified and appropriately registered Ethiopian plumber in London is registered on the platform.

The customer submits the requirement.

The platform matches the customer with the plumber.

The job is completed.

The platform earns either a commission, referral fee, lead fee, booking fee or another agreed commercial percentage.

The same model could eventually apply to:

- Plumbers
- Electricians
- Builders
- Decorators
- Architects
- Engineers
- Property agents
- Mortgage advisers
- Insurance professionals
- Surveyors
- Conveyancing/property lawyers
- Cleaners
- Handymen
- Removal companies
- Other home and property services

The architecture should therefore be **international from the outset**, even though Ethiopia remains the core market.

**Ethiopian Community → Owns property in Ethiopia&#x20;**

Regarding the diaspora community, we need to be particularly creative in how we serve those who own property in Ethiopia but live abroad. Many of these property owners are unable to manage their assets actively and, as a result, rely heavily on friends and relatives. This arrangement can often be inconvenient, uncomfortable, and unreliable.

A well-structured professional service designed specifically for this segment could address this problem very effectively. We could provide a comprehensive property management solution that manages, maintains, secures, and monitors their properties on their behalf. The service could also include tenant management, property rental, rent collection, inspections, maintenance coordination, and, where required, representation of the owner in matters relating to the property. If an owner decides to sell, we could also coordinate and manage the sales process on their behalf.

In effect, we would be creating a 24/7, 365-day property support service that revolves around protecting and managing the client's property while they are abroad. The objective would be to give diaspora property owners complete peace of mind, security, convenience, transparency, and professional support.

Most importantly, this service would remove the unnecessary dependence on friends and relatives and replace it with a reliable, accountable, and professionally managed solution. For diaspora clients, we would effectively become their trusted representative on the ground in Ethiopia, ensuring that their property is properly looked after at all times.

### 11. Property Development — Coming Soon

The business intends eventually to develop and sell its own property.

However, because the company is **not currently actively developing properties**, this section should not give customers the impression that developments already exist.

The platform should have an **Our Developments** section, but initially it should clearly display:

**Coming Soon**

## The Bigger Business Model

The important principle is that these should **not feel like ten unrelated businesses placed onto one website**.

They should function as one interconnected ecosystem.

For example:

A customer finds land through the property marketplace.

They obtain financing through a platform partner.

They find an architect through the professional marketplace.

They appoint an engineer.

They request contractor quotations.

The contractor purchases materials through the building-supplies marketplace.

The customer obtains construction insurance.

The property is built.

The owner uses the platform for ongoing repairs and maintenance.

Later, they may rent or sell the property through the property marketplace.

Meanwhile, the professionals and contractors involved may recruit staff through the recruitment section.

That is the ecosystem.

Each vertical should strengthen the others.

---

## Core Platform Positioning

The platform should therefore **not position itself simply as a construction supplier**.

It should also **not position itself simply as an estate agent**.

And it should **not position itself simply as a directory**.

The long-term position should be something closer to:

**One platform for property, construction and everything around it.**

For the Ethiopian market and Ethiopian communities internationally.

The platform should be designed so that someone can effectively:

**Find it. Buy it. Finance it. Build it. Supply it. Insure it. Maintain it. Improve it. Sell it. Rent it. Staff it.**

All through one ecosystem.

- List Your Business
- Become a Supplier
- Join as a Professional
- Post a Job
- Request a Service
- Request a Quote
- Advertise
- Help & Advice
- My Account

Not every vertical needs to be fully operational on launch day.

However, **the underlying architecture, navigation, data model and brand positioning should anticipate them from the beginning**, so that new divisions can be activated progressively without having to redesign the entire platform each time.

---

## Launch Philosophy

We should not make the mistake of trying to operationally launch every business line simultaneously.

The platform can communicate the **full ecosystem vision**, while services are introduced in phases.

The first release should concentrate on the areas where the company can genuinely transact or generate leads immediately.

Other services should use:

- Coming Soon
- Register Your Interest
- Request Information
- Join the Waiting List
- Become a Partner

where appropriate.

This allows the platform to look substantial and communicate the long-term ambition without pretending that services exist before the operational infrastructure behind them is ready.

The objective is ultimately very simple:

**If it relates to property, construction, housing or the built environment for an Ethiopian customer — whether they are in Ethiopia or abroad — we should aim to have a place for it within this ecosystem.**
