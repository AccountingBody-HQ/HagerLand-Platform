# Live journey test: your own check of the live site

**For:** AB, on the live site (https://www.hagerland.com), after "RELEASE POLISH 1".
**Time needed:** about 60–90 minutes.
**You need:** your phone (for the authenticator code), your own email inbox, and a **second email address** you can open (for example a second Gmail account). At least **three photos** of anything (a room, a wall, a view) on the computer or phone you use.

This walks through what a real owner, a buyer and a customer do on HagerLand, so you see it all working with your own eyes. Everything you create is marked **TEST – please ignore**, and the last part shows you exactly how to remove it all.

How to use it: do the steps in order. Tick each box (☐ → ☑) when you see what the step says you should see. If something looks different, write down the step number and what you saw, and send it to me.

Emails come from **HagerLand <hello@accountingbody.com>**. If one does not arrive within 5 minutes, check your spam folder before anything else.

## Part 1: Sign up as a private owner

☐ **1.1** Open https://www.hagerland.com in a private (incognito) browser window.
*You should see:* the homepage with "One platform for property, construction and everything around it." and, under it, "Everything your property needs".

☐ **1.2** Click **List with us** (top right), then **Sell or let a property**, then **List your property**.
*You should see:* the sign-up page asking "What brings you to HagerLand?".

☐ **1.3** Choose the owner option. Fill in **Full name** (your name), **Email** (your own email), **Phone** and a **Password** of at least 10 characters. Tick the terms box. Complete the "I am human" check if it appears, then press **Sign up**.
*You should see:* "Check your email".

☐ **1.4** Open your inbox. Find the email from HagerLand asking you to confirm your email address, and click the button in it.
*You should see:* you are taken back to HagerLand, signed in, with "Welcome to HagerLand".

☐ **1.5** Click your name or **My account** (top right), then **Sign out**. Then click **Sign in**, enter your email and password, and press **Sign in**.
*You should see:* your account overview again. Signing in works.

## Part 6: Reset your password

☐ **6.1** Sign out of your owner account. On the **Sign in** page, click **Forgot your password?** (or go to https://www.hagerland.com/auth/reset-password). Enter your own email and press **Send reset link**.
*You should see:* a message that a link is on its way.

☐ **6.2** Open the email and click the link.
*You should see:* a page to choose a new password.

☐ **6.3** Enter a new password twice and press **Save new password**.
*You should see:* "Your password has been updated." Sign in with the new password to be sure.

## Part 7: Clean up (so nothing test-related stays visible)

Do all of this in the admin (https://www.hagerland.com/roodber8).

☐ **7.1 The test listing (the only thing visitors could see).** Click **Properties**, open `TEST – please ignore`, press **Remove from the site** and confirm.
*You should see:* "Removed from the site." In the private window, refresh Property for sale: the listing is gone (allow a minute).

☐ **7.2 The enquiries and the contact message.** Click **Enquiries**. For each test entry, set the status to **Spam**. (Enquiries are never shown to visitors; this keeps your reports clean.)

☐ **7.3 The supplies quote.** Click **Quotes**, open the test quote request and set its status to **Cancelled**.

☐ **7.4 The service request.** Click **Service requests**, open the test request, close it and choose the reason **Spam**.

☐ **7.5 The interest registration.** Click **Interest registrations**, open the test entry, set its status to **Closed** and write `test` in the notes.

☐ **7.6 The owner test account (optional).** If you do not want to keep the owner account you made in Part 1, click **Users**, find your email, press **Delete**, and type your email to confirm. You will get an "account deleted" email. (Keeping it is fine: it is private.)

☐ **7.7 Final look.** In a private window, open the homepage, **Property for sale**, and search for `TEST`.
*You should see:* nothing marked TEST anywhere on the public site.
