# HagerLand Operations handbook

For the **Operations** team member in Addis Ababa. It covers the daily routine, the weekly tasks, how to do each job in the admin, what you must never do, and who to call.

How the admin works:
- You sign in at **www.hagerland.com/roodber8-login** → open **Staff sign in** → **Sign in as staff**, with your own email, password and the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
- The menu on the left shows only the areas the Operations role can open. A number next to a menu item means work is waiting there.
- Everything you do is recorded in the audit log under your name.
- The owner (AB) runs the site, the money, the settings and this handbook. When this handbook says "tell AB", send him a short message with the reference number (for example HLS-2026-000123); never send customers' personal details in that message.

---

## 1. Your daily routine, in this order

1. **Dashboard.** Open **Dashboard** and read the counts. Anything in red needs attention today.
   - The **daily digest email** goes to AB, not to you. If AB forwards you a line from it, deal with that first.
2. **Emergencies, then service requests.** Open **Service requests**.
   - Requests marked **Emergency** come first.
   - Then requests waiting to be sent to providers, then those where every provider declined or has not answered.
   - How: section 3.1.
3. **Supply quotes.** Open **Quotes** (in the "Customer requests" group).
   - Work on the oldest waiting quote first. A quote is valid for **3 days** from the moment it is issued.
   - How: section 3.2.
4. **Call-back requests.** Only when switched on: today **Call-back requests is switched off**, so the list stays empty.
   - When AB switches it on: open **Call-back requests** and call each person at the time they chose (Addis Ababa time).
   - How: section 3.6.
5. **Enquiries older than 48 hours, the Review queue and Email problems.**
   - The Operations role cannot open these.
   - If a customer tells you their message or listing is stuck, or that an email never arrived, tell AB with the reference.
6. **Anything AB asked you to check.**

## 2. Weekly tasks (Monday)

1. **Supplier prices running out.** **Supply partners** → **Prices expiring this week**.
   - Call each partner listed (the page shows whom to call; never call anyone marked "Do not contact").
   - Ask for their new price and record it on the partner's offers page.
2. **Partners due for re-verification.** **Supply partners** → filter **Re-verification** (dates in red are due).
   - Call the partner and confirm their details are still right, then update them on the partner's page.
3. **Job reports.** **Jobs and applications** → **Job reports**.
   - Decide every open report (section 3.5). Jobs are never paused automatically; they stay live until someone decides.

## 3. How to

### 3.1 Send a service request to providers ("match")

1. **Service requests** → open the request.
2. Read the request, the customer's area and how soon they need it. Never share the customer's details outside the admin.
3. In **Send to providers**:
   - choose suitable published businesses that work in that area (use **Find a business by name** if needed);
   - send the request to them: **up to three providers at a time**.
   - If HagerLand will do the work itself, use **Assign HagerLand's own team**.
4. Each provider gets an email and answers in their account. Their quotes then appear on the request and on the customer's request page.
5. If no provider can take it, use **No suitable provider…** and confirm: the request closes and the customer gets a polite email.
6. Add anything useful under **Notes** (private: customers and providers never see notes).

Staffing requests (people asking HagerLand to find workers) are not sent to providers: they belong to the recruitment desk (section 3.7).

### 3.2 Work on and issue a supply quote

1. **Quotes** → open the quote (reference HLQ-…).
2. Check the items and quantities.
3. Ask suppliers:
   - **Suggested suppliers** shows partners with current prices for these items;
   - choose them, call or message them, and record each reply (price and delivery) on the quote.
4. Compare the replies (the cheapest is marked).
5. When the price for the customer is ready (AB or the owner decides any margin or commission; you never see or set it):
   - fill in **Issue quote**: **Quote total (ETB, including VAT)**;
   - **Upload the quote PDF**;
   - tick **Email the customer that their quote is ready** (if the customer gave no email, the page says **No email address: call the customer**).
6. The quote shows **Valid until** (3 days later). After that it expires automatically, and the customer asks for a new one.

### 3.3 Approve or decline a listing

Properties, developments, business profiles and products waiting for approval are in the **Review queue**. **The Operations role cannot open it**: AB (owner) or an Agent decides them. If a customer asks about a listing that is waiting, tell them it is with the team and tell AB the listing's reference.

### 3.4 Record a verification check (the "Verified" badge)

1. **Organisations** → open the business → the **Verified badge** panel → **Record verification check**.
2. Record only what you actually checked yourself:
   - the trade licence;
   - the tax number (TIN);
   - the phone call to the business.
3. The badge then shows "Verified until" a date. HagerLand reminds the business 30 days before it ends.
4. Never record a check you did not do. A Verified badge tells customers that HagerLand checked these things.

### 3.5 Handle a job report ("Report this job")

1. **Jobs and applications** → **Job reports**. Each job shows its reports, how many different people reported it, and any contact details the reporter left. **Never share those contact details with the employer.**
2. Look at the job (and call the employer if needed). Then choose:
   - **Pause while we check**: the job leaves the board, and the employer gets a neutral email (never who reported it);
   - **Employer contacted**;
   - **Dismiss** (the report was not justified);
   - **Remove the job**;
   - **Restore**: on the job's admin page, brings a paused job back with its original closing date.
3. Add a staff note explaining your decision. Reporters get no reply unless HagerLand contacts them.

### 3.6 Take a call-back request (when switched on)

**Today this feature is switched off.** When AB switches it on:
1. **Call-back requests**: each row shows what they need, their language, the best time to call (Addis Ababa time) and their phone.
2. Call at that time.
3. Record what happened on the row. If the person says they never asked for a call, choose that option: their number is deleted straight away.

### 3.7 The recruitment desk

**Today the recruitment desk is switched off** (no new staffing requests reach it). **Recruitment desk** and **Worker register** in the Jobs group show earlier records only. When AB switches it on:
1. Open the assignment.
2. Add candidates.
3. Record each person's **consent** (and that they are an adult) **before** introducing them to an employer.
4. Introduce them, then record a hire as a placement.

Fees and amounts are recorded by AB only.

## 4. What Operations must never see or do

- **Money and fees.**
  - Lead fees, commissions, margins, the supply ledger and the Jobs report are owner-only. You will not see them; never ask for them or write amounts into notes.
  - Customer prices on a quote are only what you type into **Issue quote**, as AB decided.
- **Switches and settings.** Never change any switch or setting (Settings, Verticals, Pillars and modes, Staff). The Operations role cannot open them; if you think something should change, tell AB.
- **Personal details.** Never share a reporter's contact, a candidate's contact or a customer's details with anyone outside the admin, including employers, providers and suppliers, except through the site's own buttons (for example sending a request to providers). Never copy personal details into WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets or emails.
- **Never promise a time** to a customer ("today", "within 24 hours"…). Say what will happen, not when (section 6).

## 5. Who to call when

| Problem | What to check | Then |
|---|---|---|
| **The site seems down** | Open `https://www.hagerland.com/api/health` in a browser. `"ok": true` means the site is up. | No answer or not ok: wait 2 minutes and try again; still down: **call AB**. |
| **Customers say emails do not arrive** | Ask for the reference and the email address they gave (check for typing mistakes). | Tell AB: he can see undelivered emails (Email problems) and resend them. |
| **The daily job seems not to have run** (for example, quotes are not expiring, or reminders are missing) | Nothing for you to check: the daily job and its digest are AB's. | Tell AB the day and what you noticed. |
| **A customer asks to delete their data** | Do not delete anything yourself. | Tell AB **the same day**: the account owner asks from **My account → Settings → Delete account**, and AB completes it within 30 days. |
| **Anything else that looks wrong** | Write down the reference, the page address and what you saw. | Tell AB. |

## 6. Customer replies (plain language, no time promises)

Use these on the phone or in messages. Fill in the part in [brackets].

- **Service request received:** "Thank you, we have your request [HLS-…]. We are finding suitable providers in your area. Each provider who can help will contact you directly, and their quotes appear on your request page."
- **No provider available:** "We are sorry: we could not find a provider for this request at the moment. You can send a new request at any time, and we will try again."
- **Supply quote being prepared:** "Thank you, we have your quote request [HLQ-…]. We are checking prices and delivery with our suppliers and will send you the quote when it is ready."
- **Supply quote issued:** "Your quote [HLQ-…] is ready. It is valid for 3 days. Reply to the email or call us to confirm your order and delivery."
- **Quote expired:** "That quote has expired because prices change often. Send us your list again and we will prepare a new quote."
- **Listing waiting for approval:** "Your listing is with our team for review. You will get an email when it is live, or if anything needs changing."
- **Data question:** "You can download your data from My account → Settings → Download my data, and ask for your account to be deleted from the same page."

## 7. Data protection basics

- Personal details (names, phones, emails, CVs, ID documents) are only for doing the job in the admin. **Never share them.** Never take screenshots of them to send elsewhere.
- **Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.** Tell AB the same day you hear of one.
- Verification documents, applications and other private files open through links that expire after 10 minutes. Do not download them to your own phone or computer.
- Sign out of the admin on shared computers. Never share your sign-in, your password or your authenticator code. If your phone with the authenticator app is lost, tell AB at once.
